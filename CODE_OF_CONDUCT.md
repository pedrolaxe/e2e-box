# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want e2e-box to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Help newcomers: everyone started somewhere, especially with adb.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel of the project) and
to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Pedro Laxe, at pedrolaxe@gmail.com. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban
anyone who breaks this code of conduct, depending on how serious and repeated the behavior is.
